Traveling between islands

Island hopping costs most of a day

Visitors travel between islands almost entirely by air. The only scheduled passenger ferry links Maui and Lānaʻi. The flight is short, but checkout, rental cars and airport buffers turn a 40-minute hop into a half day or more.

          Packing by situation

          Arrival and getting around

          • Book rental cars early; island fleets are limited and peak periods sell out.
          • Plan a light first day. Mainland flights often land in the afternoon, and jet lag catches up by evening.
          • Leave 3 hours on your departure day between checkout and an inter-island flight, more on Hawaiʻi Island and Kauaʻi north shore.
          • Parking at popular beaches and trailheads fills early; some state parks charge nonresident parking fees.
          • Check whether a vacation rental is legally permitted; counties restrict short-term rentals in many areas.

          How we choose and check

          • Editorial picks are chosen for a specific reason (a signature dish, local history, setting or value), not by popularity alone or payment. We have no sponsored listings. If that changes, sponsored items will be labeled and kept out of editorial rankings.
          • Facts that change (hours, reservations, access, safety) come from official sources. Each card shows when it was last verified or tells you it hasn't been.
          • Ratings from review sites appear only as a secondary link and are never mixed with our recommendations.
          • History and cultural content is drafted for review by Hawaiian cultural practitioners and local historians.
