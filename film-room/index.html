How the model works

Player paths come from frames of the broadcast every 0.25–0.5 s, measured against the yard lines and hash marks. From the decision point on, each defender keeps running his path from the film until he reacts. Then he chases the ball or the runner at his top speed and acceleration. A tackle happens when a defender gets within about a yard of the runner. Every input below varies a little in each simulation.
